How to use

  1. After closing each day, photograph the POS Z report under “Close”, check the numbers and save.
  2. When stock arrives, tap the item under “Stock”, choose “Stock in” and enter the quantity and total price.
  3. Count stock once a week: tap the item, choose “Count” and enter what is actually left.
  4. At month end, fill in rent, wages and other expenses under “Report” to see net profit, and export it for the accountant.
  5. iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share → “Add to Home Screen” to use it like an app.
